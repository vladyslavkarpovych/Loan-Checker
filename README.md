# Loan-Checker

The growth of the credit market, driven by changing consumer behavior and reduced disposable income, presents financial institutions challenges. These include an increased volume of loan applications, applications from financially unstable customers, and the need for quick but accurate lending decisions.
This project aims to create a machine learning model to predict an individual’s ability to repay credit, helping financial institutions assess credit risk. The project applies TensorFlow to offer a more reliable solution for evaluating creditworthiness. Accurate predictions can reduce loan defaults and streamline credit assessments. 

•	 Who Benefits and How?
The solution can be applied within banking institutions, credit scoring agencies, and fintech companies into their loan application processes, improving decision-making, and reducing the risk of default. Individuals may benefit from more faster and accurate assessments.

•	 Why does the problem seem interesting to us?
Leveraging our work experience in financial institutions, we highlight the importance of using machine learning to strengthen credit risk assessments and increase the precision of repayment predictions.
